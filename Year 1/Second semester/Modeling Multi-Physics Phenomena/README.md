# ⚙️🔬 Modeling of Multiphysics Phenomena

The course is taught by three main professors 👨‍🏫👩‍🏫 and is divided into two primary sections:

1. **Mechanics & Fluid Dynamics 🌊:** Focuses on linear and non-linear elasticity, covering solid mechanics and fluid dynamics.
2. **Bioelectricity & Sensors ⚡:** Explores electrostimulation, sensors, and broader bioelectrical phenomena.

Both sections include practical implementations using COMSOL Multiphysics 💻.

---

## 📝 Exam Format

The exam consists of two main components:

1. **Oral Examination 🗣️:** This covers the first part of the course (elasticity and fluid dynamics). You will be expected to discuss mathematical proofs and their corresponding implementations in COMSOL.
2. **Project 🛠️:** You must develop a project in COMSOL, optionally integrating MATLAB or Python 🐍. The professor provides a list of suggested topics, but you are also free to propose your own idea 💡.

**Note 📌:** The two parts of the exam can be taken independently. The theoretical oral exam must be taken during the official exam sessions (*appelli* 📅), whereas for the project presentation, you can simply contact the professor to agree on a date together 🤝.

---

## 🎯 Tips & Tricks

* **For the oral exam 📚:** I highly recommend asking the professors for a specific list of topics to study and closely following the PDF materials they upload to Microsoft Teams 📁. As for the COMSOL portion, practicing the models a few times should be enough to perform well ✅.
* **For the project 📊:** Find one or more research papers relevant to your chosen topic and try to either replicate their model or extend it 🔬. The professor greatly appreciates a solid bibliography 📖 and a direct comparison between your simulation results and the literature during your presentation 📈.

---

## 💭 Final Considerations

* **Part 1 (Theory) 🧠:** The oral exam is heavily theoretical. I found it slightly confusing because the required study material wasn't strictly defined, so I don't have extensive advice here 🤷‍♂️. However, the overall amount of theory isn't overwhelming, so it shouldn't take too much time to prepare ⏳.
* **Part 2 (Project) 🚀:** The project is highly engaging. The professor evaluating it is very approachable 😎, doesn't expect an overly complex model, and generally doesn't ask theoretical questions during the presentation. If you put in genuine effort 💪, build a good bibliography, compare your results with existing literature, and try to apply some theoretical concepts from the course (though not mandatory), he will appreciate it and likely reward you with a high grade 🏆.
