# 🤖 Soft Robotics Technologies Exam Guide

## 📝 Exam Structure

The exam is structured into two main parts:

* **Theoretical questions on actuation technologies:** The professor will start by asking some theoretical questions focused specifically on the different actuation technologies. 

* **Ansys Simulation:** The professor will provide his own laptop with an Ansys simulation already running and will give you specific indications: boundary conditions, loads, and the different required results. Your task will be to apply them and configure the rest of the simulation. **Attention:** while proceeding, you will have to explain in detail every single step you are taking. From time to time, you will be asked to explain the different available options and the reason behind your choices.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

* **Theoretical Part:** Study directly from the notes and focus deeply on the actuation technologies. It is enough to focus on the introduction (roughly the first 40 pages) of the file **`teoria_completo.pdf`**.
* **Ansys Simulation:** Absolutely do not underestimate this part! The simulation itself is not overly complex, but the real difficulty lies in precisely explaining the different options and settings within Ansys. Study directly from the dedicated notes: you can quickly read through the first pages, but from the **Newton-Raphson method** onwards, it is crucial to know practically everything. You can find everything in the file **`ansys_notes.pdf`**.

---

## 🎓 Final Considerations

The exam itself is not overly complicated, and the professor is very kind, helpful, and collaborative. 

The golden rule is **do not underestimate the exam**: the syllabus combining the theoretical part and the Ansys simulation is neither short nor entirely trivial. 

Study well, stay calm, and good luck with the exam!
