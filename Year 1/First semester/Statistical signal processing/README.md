# 📉 Statistical signal processing  - Exam Guide

## 📋 Exam Format
The exam consists of two distinct parts. You must pass the written exam before you can access the oral.

* **The "Carry-Over" Rule:** You can pass the written exam in one session (*appello*) and take the oral in a completely different one later. The professor will keep your written grade on file.
* **The Risk:** If you fail the oral or refuse the final grade, **your written grade is voided**, and you must retake both parts.

### 📝 Written Exam
* **Structure:** Usually consists of one large, comprehensive exercise.
* **Preparation:** The exam structure is highly redundant and consistent year-to-year. Practice the provided past exams extensively.

### 🗣️ Oral Exam (The Challenge)
This is the most difficult part of the course. It follows a specific format:
1. **The Long Question:** The professor gives you a very generic theoretical topic. You have **30 minutes** to write down a detailed explanation and all relevant derivations on paper .
2. **The Discussion:** Afterward, he will discuss what you wrote and ask questions about specific graphs or examples taken directly from the lecture slides.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks
* **Written Strategy:** Straightforward—repetition is key. Solve as many past exercises as possible until the workflow becomes second nature.
* **Oral Strategy:** This requires a complete change of approach compared to the written. Focus heavily on **mathematical proofs and demonstrations** (usually found in the first half of each PDF). You are often expected to reproduce these step-by-step.
* **Time Management:** This course requires significant preparation time. Do not leave it all for the exam session; studying "in parallel" with the lectures will save you a lot of stress.

---

## 🏁 Subjective Opinion & Advice
Don't be intimidated. While the professor is kind and clearly loves the subject, the course has a few hurdles:

* **The Difficulty:** With only 6 CFU, the workload feels much heavier. The pure theory and focus on proofs might remind you of *Calculus 1*. It is normal to feel completely lost during the semester—most of us did, but we still passed.
* **Attendance Matters:** The professor values students who show up. Even if you don't understand everything in the moment, **stay in the room and endure it.** Being a familiar face at the oral exam can work in your favor—he is generally much gentler with students he recognizes(this is my opinion, so i cannot be sure).
* **The "First Session" Goal:** Try your best to clear this exam in the first winter session. It is arguably the hardest exam of the semester; if you leave it behind, it becomes very easy to procrastinate on it in favor of more "interesting" subjects.
* **The Human Side:** Be friendly! On the last day of class, he usually brings **Prosecco and Panettone** to celebrate with the students.

**Final word:** Study hard, follow the lectures, and good luck!
