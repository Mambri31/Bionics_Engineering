# 🎓 Exam Information & Tips

## 📋 Exam Format
You can complete the exam in one of two ways:
1. **Midterm Exams (*Prove in itinere*):** Divided into three separate tests held throughout the semester.
2. **Full Exam:** A single comprehensive exam held during the official exam sessions.

**Note:** The exam consists **entirely of exercises**. There are no theoretical questions.

---

## 💡 Tips & Tricks
> **Strong Recommendation:** Do the *Prove in itinere*. 

* **Difficulty:** Generally manageable. The exercises often follow the patterns of those found in this repository.
* **Consistency:** Although the professor did not release the last of the three midterm papers, the structure remained predictable. The first exercise was identical to those from previous exams, and the second covered a case study specifically explained during the lectures.
* **Grading:** The grading is quite favorable. Even if you don't perform perfectly on one of the three tests, a high average is very achievable if you do well on the others.
* **Pro-tip:** The professor prefers the midterm format over the full exam, so it is usually the "smoother" path.

---

## 🏁 Final Considerations
While these thoughts are subjective, they may help you plan your study:

* **General Difficulty:** The course is not overly difficult, especially if you have a background in the subject. If it is your first time studying these topics, expect a bit more of a challenge.
* **Study Material:** I highly suggest practicing heavily from the textbook **"Scienza delle Costruzioni"**. It provides excellent exercises for almost all topics covered in the three exams (with the exception of non-linear mechanics).
* **The Bottom Line:** I cannot stress this enough—**choose the midterm path.** It allows you to skip the stress of the full exam session and is statistically the easier route to a high grade.
